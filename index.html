UAE Visa Application Status Check Online: Complete Guide to Tracking Your Visa

Checking your UAE visa status online is an important step for anyone planning to travel to the United Arab Emirates, waiting for visa approval, or living in the country on a residence permit. Whether you have applied for a Dubai visit visa, tourist visa, employment visa, or residence visa, checking your visa information helps you understand your application progress and confirm the validity of your existing visa.

The Visa Status Check UAE process allows applicants and visa holders to access relevant information through official government services. Dubai GoGo provides helpful guidance on checking UAE visa status and finding the appropriate online service. The correct website depends on the emirate that issued the visa. Dubai visa records are generally checked through the General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA Dubai), while visa applications and validity for Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah, and Fujairah are generally checked through the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP).

This guide explains how to check UAE visa status online, verify visa validity, track an application, understand common status messages, and find the appropriate official service.

What Is a UAE Visa Status Check?

A UAE visa status check is an online process used to review information about an issued visa or a pending visa application. Depending on the service and the information available, you may be able to confirm whether a visa has been issued, review its validity, or see the progress of an application.

This service can be useful for tourists, visitors, employees, employers, sponsors, and UAE residents who want to verify their immigration documents.

A UAE visa status check can help you with several tasks:

  • Checking the progress of a submitted visa application.
  • Verifying the validity of an issued entry permit or residence visa.
  • Reviewing visa issuance and expiry information.
  • Confirming whether your application requires further attention.
  • Identifying the appropriate government authority for a visa inquiry.
  • Checking available visa records before making travel arrangements.

The information displayed depends on the official portal, the type of visa, and the details entered during the search.

How to Check UAE Visa Status Online

The easiest way to check your UAE visa status is to use the official government portal responsible for your visa. Before starting, identify whether your visa was issued through Dubai or another emirate.

Step 1: Identify the Emirate That Issued Your Visa

First, check your application receipt, entry permit, or information from your sponsor. The issuing emirate determines which immigration authority you should contact.

For Dubai-issued visas, use the GDRFA Dubai website. For visas issued in Abu Dhabi and the other emirates listed above, use ICP Smart Services.

Choosing the correct portal is important because the two authorities maintain different immigration records and provide separate online inquiry services.

Step 2: Open the Official Visa Inquiry Website

Use the appropriate official service:

  • Dubai visas: Visit the GDRFA Dubai Visa Status service.
  • Other emirates: Visit the ICP official website and navigate to the relevant visa validity or application tracking service.

These government websites are the preferred starting points for checking official visa information. Avoid entering passport details on unfamiliar websites that claim to provide immigration records.

Step 3: Select the Relevant Checking Service

Choose the service that matches your purpose. If you have already submitted an application and are waiting for a decision, look for application tracking or application status.

If your visa has already been issued and you want to confirm its expiry date or validity, select the appropriate visa validity or file information service.

The options and required fields may differ between the ICP and GDRFA websites.

Step 4: Enter the Required Information

Depending on the service, you may need details such as:

  • Application or request number.
  • Transaction or reference number.
  • Payment date for certain Dubai application inquiries.
  • Passport number and nationality, where supported.
  • Visa file number or other identifying information.
  • Date of birth or additional details requested by the portal.

Use the exact information shown on your application receipt or immigration document. Check spelling, numbers, and dates before submitting the form.

Not every portal supports every search method, so follow the instructions displayed on the official website rather than assuming that a passport number alone will always be sufficient.

Step 5: Submit Your Request and Review the Result

Complete any verification step shown on the website and submit your inquiry. If a matching record is available, review the information displayed.

For an application inquiry, check the progress indicated by the system. For a visa validity inquiry, review the relevant issuance, expiry, or validity information provided.

Save your application reference number and keep a copy of your official documents. If the portal does not return a result, verify your information and contact the relevant authority or visa service provider for assistance.

How to Check UAE Visa Validity and Expiry Date

A UAE visa validity check helps you understand whether your entry permit or residence visa remains valid according to the information recorded by the immigration authority.

Checking validity is particularly important before travelling, extending a stay, returning to the UAE, or making decisions about a residence permit.

To check your visa validity online:

  1. Identify the emirate that issued your visa.
  2. Open the relevant ICP or GDRFA Dubai service.
  3. Select the visa validity or file inquiry option.
  4. Enter the required passport, file, or personal information.
  5. Submit the inquiry and review the available validity details.

Pay close attention to the dates shown in the official record. An entry permit may have an entry deadline, while a residence visa has its own validity period. These dates serve different purposes and should not be treated as interchangeable.

If you are uncertain about the permitted length of stay, entry deadline, or effect of an expiry date, contact the relevant immigration authority before travelling or making other arrangements.

UAE Visa Application Status Check

If you recently applied for a UAE visa, you can use the relevant government portal to check whether an application record is available and review its progress.

The application tracking process differs slightly depending on where the application was submitted.

Tracking a Visa Application Through ICP

Applicants with visas processed through ICP can use its official smart services to find the relevant application tracking option.

Keep your application or request number available. You may also need additional information depending on the service. Enter the details requested by the portal and submit the inquiry to review the available result.

If you applied through a travel agency, employer, sponsor, or authorized service provider, ask them for the application reference details if you have not received them.

Tracking a Dubai Visa Application Through GDRFA

For Dubai visa applications, use the GDRFA Dubai application status or inquiry service.

Certain Dubai application inquiries require the application number, transaction number, and payment date. These details may be included on the receipt issued when the application was submitted.

Enter the requested information carefully and follow the website's instructions. If the application cannot be located, contact the service provider or GDRFA Dubai to confirm that the reference details are correct.

How to Check Dubai Visa Status Online

Dubai visa status checks are handled through GDRFA Dubai, the authority responsible for residency and foreigner affairs in the emirate.

The official GDRFA Dubai website provides information about visa and residency services, including entry permits and visa status inquiries.

To check a Dubai visa:

  1. Visit the official GDRFA Dubai website.
  2. Open the visa status or validity inquiry service.
  3. Select the relevant inquiry option.
  4. Enter the requested application, file, or personal details.
  5. Submit the form and review the result.

If you are checking a pending application, use the application tracking option rather than relying only on a visa validity inquiry.

For help with Dubai visa inquiries, the UAE government lists Amer contact numbers of 800 5111 for callers inside the UAE and +971 4 313 9999 for callers outside the UAE. Confirm the appropriate contact channel on the official government website if contact details change.

How to Check UAE Visa Status Through ICP

ICP provides online immigration services for Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah, and Fujairah.

If your visa was issued through one of these emirates, begin with the ICP official website.

Follow these steps:

  1. Open the ICP website or its Smart Services platform.
  2. Find the relevant visa validity, application tracking, or file inquiry service.
  3. Enter the required application or identification details.
  4. Complete any verification requested by the website.
  5. Review the available information and save your reference details.

The information required depends on the selected service. Some inquiries may use an application number, while others may require passport or file information.

For official assistance, ICP lists its call centre number as 600 522 222 for callers within the UAE. If you are outside the country, check the official ICP contact page for the appropriate international contact method.

Can You Check UAE Visa Status by Passport Number?

Many applicants want to check UAE visa status by passport number because it is convenient and readily available. However, passport-based checking depends on the portal and the service selected.

Some visa validity services may allow passport information to be used, while other inquiries require an application number, transaction number, file number, or additional details.

If passport-based search is available, enter your passport number, nationality, and any other requested information exactly as shown on your passport. Make sure you use the correct official portal for the issuing emirate.

If the search does not work, do not assume that your visa has been rejected or cancelled. Check the required fields, review your application receipt, and contact the relevant authority if necessary.

Understanding Common UAE Visa Status Results

The wording displayed on an online inquiry may vary according to the immigration authority and service. Understanding the general meaning of common results can help you decide what to do next.

Application Under Process

This generally indicates that the application is still being processed. It does not necessarily mean that the visa will be approved or rejected.

Check again through the official service when appropriate. If the application remains unchanged beyond the expected processing period, contact your sponsor or service provider.

Visa Issued or Approved

A result indicating issuance or approval generally means that the authority has recorded a positive outcome for the application. Review the actual visa document and any conditions attached to it before travelling.

Do not rely solely on an informal message or a screenshot when official documentation is available.

No Record Found

A result showing no record may occur because of an incorrect reference number, a mismatch in the information entered, a delay in updating records, or use of the wrong portal.

Check your application number and other details carefully. Confirm whether the visa was issued in Dubai or another emirate, and retry through the appropriate official service.

If the problem continues, ask the sponsor or application service provider to verify the reference information.

Additional Information Required

If the authority or service provider indicates that more information is needed, follow the official instructions. Contact the relevant authority if you do not understand the request or cannot submit the required information.

The exact meaning of a status message depends on the immigration service, so avoid making assumptions without confirmation.

Documents and Information to Keep Ready

Preparing your information before starting a UAE visa status check can make the process easier.

Keep the following items available when relevant:

  • A valid or previously used passport.
  • A copy of the issued visa or entry permit, if available.
  • The application or request number.
  • The transaction or reference number.
  • The payment receipt or payment date, when required.
  • Your nationality and date of birth.
  • The contact details of your sponsor, employer, or authorized visa service provider.

You may not need every item for every inquiry. Provide only the information requested by the official service, and do not share sensitive passport or application details publicly.

Why Is My UAE Visa Status Not Showing?

Sometimes an online visa inquiry does not return the expected information. Several issues may explain the problem.

Incorrect application details: A mistyped application number, transaction number, or date can prevent the system from locating a record. Compare the information with your receipt before trying again.

Wrong immigration portal: Dubai visas are generally checked through GDRFA Dubai, while visas issued in the other six emirates listed earlier are generally checked through ICP. Confirm the issuing authority before searching.

Application processing: If your application was recently submitted, its record or status may not yet be available through the selected inquiry service. Contact the provider if you need confirmation.

Different service requirements: A validity inquiry and an application tracking service may ask for different details. Choose the service that matches your purpose.

Temporary technical issues: A website may occasionally be unavailable or fail to return a result. Try again later or use the official authority's alternative contact channels.

If you continue to experience difficulties, contact your sponsor, authorized visa service provider, ICP, or GDRFA Dubai, depending on the application.

Important Tips for Checking UAE Visa Status Safely

When checking immigration information online, accuracy and privacy are essential.

Always use official government websites for visa validity and application inquiries. Avoid websites that request unnecessary personal information or promise guaranteed visa approval.

Keep your passport details, application number, and transaction receipts secure. Do not publish screenshots containing personal information on public forums or social media.

Check your visa expiry information before making travel arrangements, and confirm any uncertainty directly with the responsible immigration authority. A status inquiry is useful for reviewing available records, but it does not replace the official visa document or specific immigration advice.

If you are applying through an employer or travel agency, request your application reference number and keep it for future inquiries. This will make it easier to follow up if the status does not change as expected.

Conclusion

A UAE visa status check online is a practical way to review your visa application progress and verify the available validity information for an issued visa. The key is to select the correct official service and enter accurate application or identification details.

For Dubai visas, use GDRFA Dubai. For visas issued in Abu Dhabi and the other relevant emirates, use ICP Smart Services. If your application cannot be found or the displayed information is unclear, verify your details and contact the responsible authority or sponsor.

By checking your visa information through the appropriate official channels and keeping your documents secure, you can make more informed decisions about travel, visa validity, and residency requirements.
